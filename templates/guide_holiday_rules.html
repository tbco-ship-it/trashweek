How holiday trash pickup delays work

When a holiday lands on a weekday, cities do one of four different things with your pickup. Which one applies depends on the city, not on the holiday. This guide sorts the {{ g.n_verified }} cities whose holiday notice TrashWeek has verified into those four rules and then shows the handling each city has posted for Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day; a date the city has not posted yet is marked "Not posted yet".

The four rules

1. One-day slide {{ g.by_pol.next_day|length }} cities

The holiday and every later pickup that week run one day later. A Thursday holiday moves Thursday routes to Friday and Friday routes to Saturday; Monday to Wednesday routes are not affected. 2. Skipped pickup, no slide {{ g.by_pol.skip|length }} cities

The pickup that falls on the holiday is not made up and the rest of the week stays put. Your next pickup is on your regular day, so a missed pickup means holding the trash until then.
