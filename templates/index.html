Why the day depends on your block, not your city

Cities run collection in routes. Your neighbor across a main road can be on a different day, and a single ZIP code usually spans three or four routes. City websites publish this as a map or a lookup tool; we pull the same route data and turn it into one page per zone that you can bookmark or add to your calendar.

Holidays

Most cities skip collection on a handful of holidays and run one day late for the rest of that week. The exact list differs by city — see which holidays delay pickup, then check your city's notice for the current week.
